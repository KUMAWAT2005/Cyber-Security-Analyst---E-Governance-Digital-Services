Week 1 — Strategic Planning for Cyber Security in E-Governance
Overview
This task develops a practical cybersecurity strategy for a representative Indian e-governance environment. The report focuses on protecting digital public services and sensitive government information while maintaining service availability, accessibility, privacy, and public trust.

The proposed strategy covers citizen-facing websites and mobile applications, APIs, identity and administrator accounts, databases and document repositories, hosting infrastructure, backups, service providers, and connections with other platforms. It is a strategic planning document—not an assessment or penetration test of an actual government department.
Objectives
- Identify cyber threats that could affect digital public services and citizen information.
- Assess risks by considering likelihood, impact, and priority.
- Recommend preventive, detective, and corrective security controls.
- Establish incident response and service-continuity practices.
- Propose a phased implementation roadmap with responsibilities, resource considerations, and measurable indicators.
- Connect the strategy with relevant Indian digital-governance guidance and recognized cybersecurity frameworks.
Key Areas Covered
- Threat landscape: account compromise, web/API exploitation, ransomware, denial-of-service, personal-data exposure, insider misuse, supplier compromise, cloud misconfiguration, and service-dependency failures.
- Risk assessment: a qualitative likelihood-and-impact method and a prioritised risk register.
- Security controls: multi-factor authentication (MFA), least-privilege access, secure software development, vulnerability management, encryption, centralised logging and monitoring, endpoint protection, network segmentation, supplier assurance, and protected backups.
- Security architecture: a proposed logical security architecture for layered protection.
- Incident response and continuity: response procedures, escalation, recovery, and service-resilience planning.
- Governance and measurement: ownership, resource allocation, performance indicators, and recurring reviews.
